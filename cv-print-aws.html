About

Software architect and engineering leader with 20+ years building large-scale distributed platforms. At Bedrock I help shape the architecture of a streaming platform fully hosted on AWS — Lambda, DynamoDB, Aurora, ElastiCache, EventBridge, CloudFront, S3 and more — serving 45M+ users under heavy traffic. I pair deep hands-on architecture with the ability to enable teams and clients: I have worked as a consultant, I orchestrate Claude (Anthropic) agents to increase engineering efficiency, and I share what I learn as an AFUP president, conference speaker and technical writer. Pragmatic, quick to pick up new technologies, and happiest turning complex problems into simple, reliable systems.
